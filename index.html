Chef Maurizio Terracciano's menu is shaped by years of travel and inspired by Mediterranean culinary heritage. The plates are designed to be simple, generous, and easy to share, creating a warm, social atmosphere at the table. Mamma Mia brings together Italian coastal cooking and modern Mediterranean sensibility, with bright Riviera flavors, gluten-free options, fresh vegetables, fish, meat dishes, family-friendly kids' meals, and daily seasonal specials.
